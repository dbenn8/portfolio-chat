# Dan Bennett — Career Background

## Overview

Dan Bennett is an AI Product Builder based in Haarlem, Netherlands (about 20 minutes from Amsterdam by train). He is a US citizen. His career spans over 15 years of product management, solution architecture, and enterprise consulting, with a consistent thread of process improvement running through every chapter. Since late 2025, he has been building production AI systems full-time — shipping software directly rather than writing specs for engineers.

## Career Timeline

### AI Product Builder (2022–Present)

**Co-Founder, Burrfect, Inc.**

Dan co-founded Burrfect, a consumer espresso tracking app available on iOS and Android. He built the AI catalog enhancement pipeline and evaluation harness, and served as the product manager for the app's 12-model recommendation engine. Burrfect is built on Firebase Cloud Functions (Node.js), with Sentry monitoring, Mixpanel analytics, and Mailchimp for user lifecycle emails.

Since December 2025, Dan has shipped over 20 production AI systems. These include n8n workflow suites, autonomous agent pipelines, local LLM infrastructure, and browser automation tooling. December 2025 was a turning point — AI coding tools reached the point where he could build directly instead of managing the spec-to-engineer handoff. He describes himself as "not a real developer — I'm an AI builder," meaning he leverages AI-assisted development tools to ship production software at speed.

### Product Manager, ServiceTitan (2017–2022)

Dan spent five years at ServiceTitan, joining when the company was valued at roughly $300M and departing during a growth phase that would eventually reach a $10B valuation (ServiceTitan is now publicly traded on NASDAQ). His focus was new market strategy: researching adjacent verticals, building competitive analysis frameworks, and coordinating cross-functional teams. This role gave him deep experience in product management at scale inside a high-growth enterprise SaaS company.

### Product Manager, ServiceMax / GE Digital (2016–2017)

At ServiceMax (later acquired by GE Digital), Dan worked on schedule optimization, dispatch systems, and internal tooling. This was a continuation of his field service management expertise, now at the product management level.

### Partner Enablement Architect, ServiceMax (2014–2016)

Dan served as a solution architect-level expert, delivering enablement services for systems integrator partners on the Salesforce platform. He bridged the gap between technical implementation and partner success.

### Professional Services Consultant, ServiceMax (2011–2014)

Dan led enterprise implementations on the Force.com platform. This hands-on consulting work built his foundation in enterprise software delivery, client management, and complex system configuration.

### Founder, FSI Consulting (2008–2016)

Dan founded and ran FSI Consulting, a consultancy focused on management, business development, systems integration, and process design. This entrepreneurial chapter ran in parallel with his ServiceMax roles and gave him experience running a business end-to-end.
